How to Play

1. Split into teams and pass this phone around.

2. On your turn, one player secretly peeks at a WHO card and a HOW card — a character and their ridiculous demise.

3. Act it out! No talking, no sounds, no pointing at objects.

4. Your team must guess the underlined words before the clock runs out. Each card guessed = 1 point.

5. First team to the target score wins the funeral… er, game. 🪦
